About the Anime

Hunter x Hunter (2011) is a Japanese anime adapted from the manga of the same name by Yoshihiro Togashi. It was animated by Madhouse and aired from October 2011 to September 2014 with a total of 148 episodes. It is a remake of the 1999 version and follows the manga more closely.

  • Genre: Action, Adventure, Fantasy
  • Episodes: 148
  • Studio: Madhouse
  • Based on: Manga by Yoshihiro Togashi
  • Rating: Fan favorite, often in top anime lists

Synopsis

Gon Freecss is a 12-year-old boy who lives on Whale Island with his aunt Mito. He believes his father is dead, until he learns that Ging is a famous Hunter, a person who travels the world to find rare treasures, creatures and people. Gon decides to become a Hunter too so he can find his dad.

He takes the very difficult Hunter Exam and meets Killua, a former assassin kid, Kurapika, the last survivor of the Kurta clan, and Leorio, who wants to be a doctor. Along the way they learn Nen, a power that uses a person's life energy, and face dangerous enemies like Hisoka and the Phantom Troupe. The story is about friendship, growing up, and the price of power.
